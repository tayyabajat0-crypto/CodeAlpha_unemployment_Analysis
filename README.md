**CodeAlpha_UnemploymentAnalysis**

**Project Description**
This project analyzes unemployment in India and shows the effect of Covid-19. It is Task 2 of my CodeAlpha Data Science internship.

**Dataset**
Unemployment in India dataset (from Kaggle). It has monthly unemployment rates for Indian states from 2019 to June 2020.

**Steps**
1. Loaded and cleaned the data (removed empty rows, fixed dates).
2. Plotted the unemployment rate over time with a line for the Covid lockdown.
3. Compared the average rate before and after the lockdown.
4. Found the 10 states with the highest unemployment after the lockdown.
5. Checked for seasonal patterns by month.

**Key Findings**
- Unemployment was stable at about 9% to 10% before the lockdown.
- After the lockdown on 25 March 2020, it jumped to about 24% to 25% in April and May.
- The average rate went from about 9.5% to about 17.8%.
- [State name] had the highest unemployment after the lockdown.
- It started to recover in June 2020.
- The data ends in June 2020, so the long-term effect is not shown.
